Alpha Group passwords must be at least {{ policy.minLength }} characters and combine upper case, lower case, numbers and symbols. Every rule below is enforced on the server as well as in your browser.

{{ policy.minLength }} Minimum characters
{{ policy.minUppercase }} Uppercase letter (A–Z)

Allowed

  • Symbols such as ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) _ + - ?
  • Passphrases, e.g. Wolf Runs@Dawn27!
  • Password-manager generated strings

Rejected

  • Common words such as password, welcome, admin
  • Sequences such as abcd or 1234, and aaa repeats
  • Your own name, e-mail or company name
